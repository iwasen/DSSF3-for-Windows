Adjustment before a measurement
  1. Volume setting
  2. Soundcard measurement
If the Realtime Analyzer is used without any adjustment, performance of the sound device (noise, distortion, and frequency response) of your PC affects the measurement. It may become critical when you use the built-in sound chip, because most of them do not have sufficient performance for sound measurement. It is important to check the sound performance of your PC and adjust it correctly before the measurement. It is also helpful to understand the internal mechanism of PC's sound device and the functions of the software.
